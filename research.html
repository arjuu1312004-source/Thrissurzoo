Bilateral Submission Rules

  • Endorsement Bounds

    Applications must strictly include an official scanned bonafide letter issued by your university department head.

  • Methodology Tracking

    Enclosure data synopses must explicitly detail your proposed sampling intervals, spatial hardware needs, and biosafety tracks.

  • Statutory Compliance

    Field experiments remain completely bounded under statutory Central Zoo Authority (CZA) rules and state canopy security protocols.
