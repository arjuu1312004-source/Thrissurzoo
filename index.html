Photography & Videography Code

  • Personal mobile photography is permitted free of charge throughout public visitor pathways.
  • SLR / DSLR camera equipment requires a valid configuration pass layout clearance (₹350 for Still Camera & 750 for Video Camera *Subject to change*).
  • Commercial filmmaking, drone tracking grids, and stationary tripods require advance written approval from the Director's Office.
  • Flash photography is strictly forbidden near animal enclosures to prevent ethological stress.

Visitor Safety & Park Regulations

  • Do not litter. Thrissur Zoological Park operates a strict zero-plastic mandate. Use designated green bins.
  • Crossing sanctuary security barriers or attempting to feed or tease fauna is a severe statutory offense.
  • Always stay centered along the brick-paved visitor lanes. Do not step into boundary forest buffers.
  • Large backpacks must be deposited at the gate complex cloakroom matrix before entry screening.
